## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, forward-thinking, and clean institutional identity for an applied sciences and vocational university. Rooted in engineering precision, technical craftsmanship, and modern academic rigor, the visual language balances the dignity of higher education with the dynamic momentum of technological industry readiness.

The aesthetic philosophy draws on **Corporate Modernism** fused with **Academic Editorial Precision**:
- **Clarity and Integrity:** Generous whitespace, razor-sharp typographic hierarchy, and structured layout grids instill trust in prospective students, industry partners, and scholars.
- **Applied Tech Pragmatism:** Functional information architecture devoid of unnecessary ornamentation, prioritizing legibility, rapid program discovery, and direct student services access.
- **Institutional Dignity:** Deep maritime blues evoke stability and academic authority, while disciplined pops of crimson convey energy, pride, and urgency.

## Colors

The color palette reflects the institutional heritage of the university emblem while maintaining strict compliance with WCAG AAA accessibility across interfaces.

### Role Assignments & Application
- **Primary Navy (`#123B8B`):** The institutional anchor. Used for primary navigation headers, major section backdrops, primary brand buttons, and authoritative headlines.
- **Royal Blue (`#174FC8`):** The interactive driver. Applied to focused states, active tabs, secondary interactive CTAs, and key links.
- **Bright Blue (`#3679E8`):** Informational highlights, status indicators, subtle accent rings, and hover transitions.
- **Accent Red (`#E52D3D`):** Sourced directly from the core gear emblem. Used sparingly for high-priority admissions badges, registration deadlines, alert notifications, and vital calls-to-action (e.g., "Daftar PMB Sekarang").
- **Dark Text Neutral (`#17243B`):** Deep ink-slate providing optimal reading contrast against white and light surfaces without the visual harshness of pure black.
- **Muted Gray (`#68758B`):** Secondary metadata, breadcrumbs, caption typography, and inactive icon states.
- **Border Gray (`#E1E7F0`):** Crisp structural dividers, card outlines, and form field perimeters.
- **Light Blue-Gray Surface (`#F3F6FC`):** Background fill for alternate content bands, card containers, and academic program directories.
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`):** High-level card surfaces, modals, hero content containers, and reading canvas.

## Typography

The type system is powered by **Inter**, chosen for its tall x-height, neutral geometric construction, and legibility at micro scales. It conveys institutional clarity, technical precision, and academic credibility.

### Usage Standards
- **Headings (`display-hero`, `headline-*`):** Rendered in bold or extra-bold weights (`700` and `800`) with tighter negative letter-spacing to enforce solid editorial authority.
- **Body Text (`body-*`):** Rendered in `#17243B` with comfortable line heights (`1.5` to `1.6`) ensuring prolonged reading comfort for academic course specifications, research summaries, and institutional articles.
- **Labels & Badges (`label-*`):** Optimized with uppercase and slight positive letter-spacing (`0.02em` to `0.04em`) to cleanly organize program accreditations, study level badges (e.g., "D3 / D4"), and metadata timestamps.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a flexible 12-column grid system paired with an 8pt mathematical rhythm. It balances academic rigor with structured information density.

### Breakpoints & Responsive Behavior
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column structure with `margin-mobile` (16px) and `gutter-mobile` (16px). Single-column card stacking for curriculum overviews, news feeds, and program paths.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8-column layout with 24px margins and gutters. Cards display in 2-column configurations; navigation collapses to a responsive drawer.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout capped at a maximum container width of `1280px` (`max-w-7xl`), centered with generous vertical padding between sections (80px to 120px) to distinguish institutional sectors (Admissions, Academics, Vocational Research, Campus Life).

## Elevation & Depth

To maintain a crisp, modern, academic tech atmosphere, this design system relies on **low-contrast structural outlines combined with ambient, cool-tinted micro-shadows**. Heavy drop shadows are omitted in favor of structural lightness.

### Depth Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat):** Used for base page canvas (`#FFFFFF` or `#F3F6FC`).
- **Level 1 (Subtle Outline Surface):** Standard academic cards, laboratory overviews, and article grids. Structured with a 1px border of `#E1E7F0`, pure white surface, and an ambient lift: `0px 2px 8px -2px rgba(18, 59, 139, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover & Elevated Blocks):** Hovered course cards, focused form panels, and floating notification banners. Border subtly shifts to `#174FC8` with a diffused elevation: `0px 8px 24px -4px rgba(18, 59, 139, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Overlay Layers):** Top navigation bars, modals, student portal dropdowns, and mega menus. Uses backdrop blur with semi-transparent surfaces (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.94)`) anchored by `0px 16px 36px -6px rgba(23, 36, 59, 0.12)` and a crisp `#E1E7F0` baseline divider.

## Shapes

The design uses a refined rounded shape language (`roundedness: 2`) that brings contemporary software ergonomics to an academic institution:

- **Cards & Content Blocks:** Utilize `rounded-xl` (16px / `1rem`) and `rounded-2xl` (24px / `1.5rem`) for major program containers, showcase banners, and feature modules.
- **Interactive Controls:** Buttons, inputs, and tabs apply an intentional `rounded-xl` or medium `0.75rem` (12px) curve, establishing a modern vocational software feel.
- **Badges & Tags:** Utilize full pill radiuses (`rounded-full`) for status indicators (e.g., "Akreditasi A", "Pendaftaran Dibuka") to create visual distinction from angular structural containers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Admissions / Portal):** Solid background of `#123B8B`, text in `#FFFFFF`, font weight `600`, radius `rounded-xl`, padding `0.75rem 1.5rem`. On hover, background shifts to `#174FC8` with a micro translateY(-1px) elevation.
- **Accent Action (Crucial Deadlines / CTA):** Solid `#E52D3D` with `#FFFFFF` text. Used exclusively for primary conversion targets such as "Daftar Sekarang" (PMB).
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background transparent, 1.5px border `#E1E7F0`, text `#123B8B`. On hover, background transitions to `#F3F6FC` and border shifts to `#174FC8`.
- **Ghost Button:** Text `#174FC8`, no border, padding `0.5rem 1rem`. For inline text actions and table drill-downs.

### Cards & Editorial Modules
- **Program / Study Path Card:** White surface, 1px solid `#E1E7F0` border, `rounded-2xl` border-radius. Internal padding of `1.5rem` (`space-lg`). Includes a top tag for degree type (e.g., "D3 Teknik", "D4 Terapan"), prominent bold title (`headline-sm`), brief description in `#68758B`, and a footer link with arrow icon.
- **News & Event Card:** Clean image container at a 16:9 ratio with `rounded-xl` top masking, date pill floating at the top left, followed by headline in `#17243B` and metadata row.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status & Accreditation Badges:** `rounded-full` pill structure with `0.25rem 0.75rem` padding.
  - *Accredited / Active:* Background `rgba(54, 121, 232, 0.1)`, text `#174FC8`, border `1px solid rgba(54, 121, 232, 0.2)`.
  - *Urgent / Deadline:* Background `rgba(229, 45, 61, 0.1)`, text `#E52D3D`, border `1px solid rgba(229, 45, 61, 0.2)`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Base State:** Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #E1E7F0`, corner radius `rounded-xl`, padding `0.75rem 1rem`, text `#17243B`, placeholder `#68758B`.
- **Focus State:** Border shifts to `#174FC8` with an ambient focus ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(23, 79, 200, 0.15)`).

### Academic Program Navigator & Tabs
- Horizontal tab list on a `#F3F6FC` segmented container. Active item renders as a pure white card with subtle elevation and `#123B8B` bold text; inactive items render in `#68758B` with clean hover feedback.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size `1.25rem` square/circle, border `1.5px solid #E1E7F0`. When selected, fills with `#123B8B` displaying crisp white vector marks.
